# MomentumBot progress — September 8, 2026

MomentumBot has a substantial research engine and a verified historical data
foundation for its first narrow strategy. We are finishing the integration
needed for a credible chronological account backtest. The project has not yet
established profitability, reliable paper operation or full Ross Cameron
strategy coverage.

## What this step completed

The management exit source bundle now combines all 80 newly captured sources
with the two original XAGE sources. Its 41 quote/status pairs contain 2,223,448
records. The composer preserves the complete original bytes, request identities
and quote ordinals; its reader enforces each original opportunity's bounds.
The primary and independent verifiers agree, and reader boundary verification
covers all 109 original opportunities. No additional market-data request or
purchase was needed.

All 23 unavailable entry classifications remain unavailable. The other 86 have
available entry inputs; this does not mean 86 executed trades. Account limits,
order execution and later management still determine actual trading activity.

## Remaining path to the first meaningful account result

1. Bind historically correct 2025 fees and reconcile executable sell fills into
   account cash, positions, risk, buying power and cumulative costs.
2. Produce authenticated preceding-account state, preserve unresolved positions
   and orders, and value carried positions using only information available at
   the next session's required time. Account seeds must never reset daily.
3. Integrate continuous chronological order/account/scarcity handling and bind
   all verified inputs in the historical execution registration.
4. Run all registered account paths across all 30 sessions and freeze the full
   state chain, including failures, unavailable inputs and open positions.
5. Evaluate the frozen result, then open retrospective Ross evidence for the
   registered comparison. Further held-out and prospective paper evidence is
   needed before promoting any policy.

The practical milestone is an end-to-end, causally valid account result that
we can inspect and reproduce. Engineering completion and evidence of a trading
edge are different measures; assigning one percentage to both would be misleading.
