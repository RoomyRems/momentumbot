# MomentumBot progress — September 8, 2026

MomentumBot has a substantial research engine and a verified historical data
foundation for its first narrow strategy. We are finishing the integration
needed for a credible chronological account backtest. The project has not yet
established profitability, reliable paper operation or full Ross Cameron
strategy coverage.

## What this step completed

Original market-source binding is implemented and independently verified locally.
Every historical decision now resolves to its exact scanner candidate and
original entry, management and exit sources. All 109 opportunities and 360
account-session slots remain present, including all unavailable inputs. This
completes source authentication at the account boundary; the first historical
account execution remains the next development step.

The carry map explicitly records missing corporate-action continuity, next-day
valuation inputs and execution beyond expired windows. These gaps will block
affected continuation while preserving known state. No source-binding result
is a completed account backtest or evidence of profitability.

All 2,180 local tests passed with zero skips. The 135-test focused group passed
normally and optimized. Independent verification covered every candidate and
context, all 218 management streams (6,641,822 envelope occurrences), 89 entry
sources, 41 exit pairs and 348 carry dependencies. Hosted reproduction is the
remaining publication check. The [component record](../research/sealed_historical_source_binding_v01.md)
and [audit](../../research/data-audits/sealed-historical-source-binding-v0.1-independent-verification.json)
contain the exact commitments and evidence.

Positions still open when a source window expires remain explicit. Their next-day

## Previous step: fee and confirmed-fill reconciliation

## Remaining path to the first meaningful account result

1. Register original market and corporate-action source binding for the complete
   account engine, retaining expired-window carry and missing valuations explicitly.
   New execution windows or retry rules cannot be inferred from a carried position.
2. Bind and independently verify those original inputs without changing the
   frozen strategy, risk, fees or registered opportunity coverage.
3. Run all registered account paths across all 30 sessions and freeze the full
   state chain, including failures, unavailable inputs and open positions.
4. Evaluate the frozen result, then open retrospective Ross evidence for the
   registered comparison. Further held-out and prospective paper evidence is
   needed before promoting any policy.

The practical milestone is an end-to-end, causally valid account result that
we can inspect and reproduce. Engineering completion and evidence of a trading
edge are different measures; assigning one percentage to both would be misleading.
