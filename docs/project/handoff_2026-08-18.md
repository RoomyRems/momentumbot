## Objective in plain English

Build a historically honest approximation of Ross Cameron's small-cap momentum process, then validate it in simulation before paper trading. The goal is not merely to detect a ten-second chart pattern. The eventual system must combine:

- point-in-time stock discovery and the five selection pillars;
- catalyst substance and credibility;
- which stock is becoming the obvious market leader;
- broader theme and hot/cold market context;
- daily-chart resistance and prior trading history;
- Micro pullback geometry and trigger timing;
- account state, buying power, divided attention and campaign state;
- realistic fills, latency and broker constraints; and
- Level 2/time-and-sales evidence where available and necessary.

The intended end state is hybrid. Facts, timestamps, calculations, orders and risk remain deterministic. AI may eventually help with genuinely semantic judgments, but it begins shadow-only, consumes time-causal structured evidence, may abstain, cannot place orders and cannot increase risk.

## What is implemented versus missing

Implemented and validated:

- transcript/evidence discipline and chronology guards;
- point-in-time identity, universe, market qualification, float and publication-timed news;
- causal full-membership scanner rank and feature snapshots;
- deterministic scanner source-input replay sidecars;
- frozen Micro-v0.1 setup and SIP replay;
- descriptive catalyst timing/evidence and attention/leadership shadows;
- ten-session label-blind held-out runtime;
- conservative account-scoped retrospective labels; and
- no-retuning component comparison.

Still missing before a meaningful portfolio backtest:

- a frozen context-assessment policy that decides among candidates;
- causal daily-chart levels/history and theme/regime state in the same decision snapshot;
- account buying power, account-specific constraints and divided attention;
- campaign/position state so repeated Micro emissions are not counted as separate independent trades;
- complete entry/scale/exit behavior and realistic latency/slippage/broker rules;
- Level 2 and time-and-sales state or validated approximations;
- a larger preregistered representative walk-forward panel;
- live shadow calibration; and
- a separate explicit safety gate before paper authority or live money.

The project is promising as a research architecture, not yet as proof of Ross-level trading performance. The scanner acquires most observed traded symbol-days and Micro participates in many acquired trades, while the new comparison clearly identifies where context changes behavior. That is constructive. It also shows why a chart-only backtest now would overstate readiness.

## Recommended next sequence

### 1. Freeze a causal context-assessment protocol

Do this next. Do not alter Micro-v0.1.

Build a versioned structured decision snapshot at candidate activation and updates. Deterministic fields should include:

- rank/leadership transitions and persistence;
- provider-news chronology and exact evidence packet;
- daily levels, moving-average resistance and recent failed-pop history where point-in-time data permits;
- theme/regime evidence available by decision time;
- price, volume, RVOL, float, spread/liquidity proxies and time of day;
- account/buying-power state when available; and
- whether another candidate already owns attention or capital.

Semantic axes may be assessed by an AI shadow reviewer:

- catalyst substance and specificity;
- proposal/authorization versus completed transaction;
- credibility and possible recycled promotion;
- theme fit and whether no-news movement is currently accepted by the market;
- opportunity obviousness/leadership quality; and
- ambiguous chart/context cleanliness.

The AI schema must cite the evidence IDs it received, distinguish observed fact from inference, include confidence and `abstain`, expire after a bounded time, and have no order/risk-increase authority.

### 2. Evaluate on a new out-of-sample panel

Register dates before opening their recaps. Run deterministic and AI context shadows label-blind, freeze them, then label and compare. Do not fit the ten sessions in this handoff.

### 3. Add campaign, portfolio and account state

Consolidate repeated Micro plans into one candidate campaign. Model whether the account already holds a position, remaining buying power, small-versus-main account constraints, simultaneous opportunities, daily P&L state and stop-trading rules.

### 4. Add realistic execution

Model broker/order latency, spread, partial fills, slippage, order cancellation/replacement, entry revalidation, scale-ins/outs and exits. Do not interpret historical P&L until this exists.

### 5. Add Level 2/tape where feasible

Historical coverage may be incomplete, so document approximations. For eventual paper/live operation, capture Level 2 and time-and-sales directly, derive deterministic absorption/liquidity/velocity summaries, and first evaluate any AI interpretation in live shadow. Large-model inference should not sit in the per-tick critical path.

### 6. Larger walk-forward, then paper

Only after the layers above are integrated should the project run a larger preregistered walk-forward portfolio backtest. If it is stable, move through historical shadow -> live shadow -> paper advisory -> paper authority. Live money requires a later explicit decision and is not implied by paper success.
