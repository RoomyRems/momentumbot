## Assessment policy

- Catalyst substance distinguishes provider-relative no-event, generic mover/roundup material, direct issuer events with incomplete terms, quantified direct events, and mixed evidence.
- Commitment distinguishes exploration/proposal, authorization without execution, definitive/completed action, withdrawal/cancellation and ambiguity.
- Credibility/repetition always abstains because the snapshot lacks filing corroboration and issuer-event history.
- Theme fit/no-news acceptance always abstains because cohort and provider-news counts do not establish a semantic issuer-theme mapping.
- Leadership uses only the frozen cross-sectional attention record and abstains when the exact candidate bar is unavailable.
- Chart context uses only prior completed daily sessions. It records close overhead, a preregistered recent failed-pop pattern, clear room or mixed evidence and abstains when daily evidence is unavailable.

Every assessed axis has an evidence citation, a separate observed-fact claim, an inference, confidence and a logical expiry no more than 300 seconds after decision time. Every strategy output remains `null`.
