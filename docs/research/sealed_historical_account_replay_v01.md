# Original historical account replay v0.1

The isolated child of source-binding audit commit
`72a67b193ed44b3e9ee39d6bbb67ceb27c5c6248` activates the original historical
sources through the frozen account-continuity mechanics. The hypothesis is
that the authenticated sources either produce a causally reconciled account
path or preserve the precise state and evidence that blocks completion.

The source-binding freeze is
`a5765974223b6551dcb6e2dffcc0f1f050c172592d6530e94013ffa6c0fbed79`;
the independently verified original binding manifest is
`9bb780dcbfbec3ef1b4e437cc3dc76e1528897956490d4ddd32d399ed5f683cd`.
All five original archives, ancestor implementation and audits are pinned.

## Registered scope

All 12 main/small account, 1/5/10-second and conservative/stress paths retain
their original 30 slots: 360 sessions and 744 opportunity references, including
162 unavailable references to 23 unique unavailable opportunities. Main starts
with $30,000 and small with $2,000 once per path. No account is reseeded after
a failure or an expired source window.

The only new runtime component resolves the original source descriptors into
the frozen scheduler. Candidate priority, Micro decisions, prices, timing,
position sizing, risk limits, two-entry campaigns, fees, order lifecycles and
execution attempt ceilings remain in their unchanged parent implementations.
The parent's synthetic-only public entry points retain their original guards.

Original management envelopes stream through the frozen cursors and complete
stream commitments. Completed bars, decisions, prints, feedback and boundaries
retain the parent's exact ordering. Execution outcomes stay private until the
existing feedback clock exposes them. The child records incremental source
progress without retaining a second full in-memory market tape.

## Unavailable inputs and carry

Unavailable original entries remain explicit, even if common exit sources have
quotes. An unavailable reference cannot establish no-trade evidence. The frozen
flat-cash policy can proceed past nonblocking unavailable inputs, but the path
remains incomplete for full account evaluation.

Each next-session record binds the exact previous close and the original carry
dependency. Open shares, cost basis, pending orders, management stops, attempts,
consumed liquidity, cash and unreconciled feedback remain in the preceding
runtime and close chain. Blocked sessions preserve that state and execute no
orders. No corporate-action continuity, opening mark, overnight window,
liquidation or retry is inferred. Historical activation does not add those
missing sources.

The outer child artifact authenticates historical source/account execution.
Nested frozen component artifacts retain their original local boundary flags;
those flags are not rewritten to imply a broader component certification.
Financial metrics, completed account-close evidence, strategy promotion and
broker-statement equivalence remain ineligible.

## Reproducibility and evidence

Registration pins the child code, checker, tests and workflow before original
replay. The builder opens only five retained archives under the offline IO
guard, writes an exclusive attempt receipt before source access, and freezes
all path results in a new output directory. A fatal attempt is retained with
its registration and failure type. Per-session failures remain in the path.

The independent stdlib checker uses the byte-pinned original binding artifact
and frozen independent chronology/accounting verifiers. It checks all slots,
candidate ordering, public reservations, confirmed shares, exact cash, fee
accrual, net guards, stream commitments, carry chains and completeness flags.
It does not claim a second independent fill simulator.

Validation includes unchanged-parent state parity, all 12 seed/slot paths,
streaming equivalence, partial and unconfirmed carry, unavailable/omitted
inputs, wrong-source rejection, and independent rejection of altered cash,
stream hashes, open-window evidence and financial promotion. The initial
synthetic parity test exposed expected child/parent close-hash differences in
blocked-state provenance. The test now translates only those wrapper hashes
and still requires exact equality of all account values and events; no runtime
policy was changed in response.

The first registration reconstruction also caught a set passed to the canonical
JSON comparator. Sorted inventory names now provide the same exact membership
check using JSON-compatible values. Both development failures precede original
replay; their logs and the superseded draft registration are retained.

Ross attachments, fills, recaps, retrospective labels and later session facts
remain prohibited from runtime. Comparison requires its separate gate after
the runtime artifact is frozen. No provider or brokerage access is part of
this workflow.

The outcome and local/hosted verification commitments will be recorded here
after the registered first execution is frozen.
