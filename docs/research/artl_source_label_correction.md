# ARTL source-label correction

## What was wrong

The full timestamped March 27 transcript proves that the old ARTL benchmark joined two different stock discussions.

- The approximately **$5.25** ten-second micro pullback, topping-tail rejection and roughly **$2,400 loss** occur during the earlier NCO/ONCO discussion.
- ARTL is discussed later, after separate breaking news. Ross says his first ARTL trade was approximately **$6.34**.

The independent market research that identified ARTL as the session's $3-to-$12.45 leader was valid. What was not valid was carrying the earlier stock's trade labels over to ARTL.

## Correction

`ross-artl-2026-03-27-micro-01` is retired from scored Micro comparisons. The corrected source describes a pullback/dip-and-curl break, but it does not resolve the exact chart timeframe or pullback ordinal. Assigning a replacement score would therefore invent evidence.

ARTL remains valuable context evidence. Ross initially distrusted it because recent pops had failed and older company headlines felt repeatedly promotional. He changed his mind only after a new shelf-registration withdrawal reduced immediate offering/dilution concern and price action confirmed demand.
