## Purpose

This is the first out-of-sample pilot for `discretion-context-assessment-shadow-v0.1`. It is designed to test whether causal deterministic context can be materialized and whether an AI shadow can produce evidence-cited assessments or appropriate abstentions.

It cannot fit a threshold, retune Micro-v0.1, produce an aggregate context score, promote a policy, establish profitability, or support a full Ross-imitation claim.
