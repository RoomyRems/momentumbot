# Strategy and discretion coverage v0.1

## Purpose

`strategy-discretion-coverage-v0.1` is an anti-overclaim inventory. It maps every one of the 36 currently promoted evidence-backed rules exactly once, then records important system components that have no promoted rule or adequate implementation.

The matrix changes no scanner, setup, entry, exit, size, risk, account, or AI behavior. Its content SHA-256 is `3507642f70bbb8f4551238bc09242dd8c31474b463bf9a4e88f03a7894d97fe3`.

## Plain-English finding

MomentumBot has a strong causal research foundation and a frozen Micro-v0.1 technical runtime. It does **not** yet contain all of Ross Cameron's strategies or discretion.

Only the first/early pullback and fast-chart micro-pullback families currently have promoted, current-era machine rules. Half-dollar and whole-dollar behavior is contextual only. Hidden-buyer anticipation and hidden-seller absorption are blocked on order-book data. Other setup families require a separate corpus-wide evidence inventory and individual registrations before they can be described as implemented.

This distinction matters because adding several loosely inferred patterns would increase activity without proving behavioral fidelity or edge.

## Coverage summary

The 25 domains include candidate selection, attention, momentum phase, setups, position and risk behavior, management, catalyst and context, account state, execution, Level 2/tape, cross-account attention, and validation.

## Highest-leverage gaps

1. **Complete setup-family inventory.** Promote current normative evidence one family at a time. Keyword hits and isolated observed trades cannot create a strategy rule.
2. **Level 2 and tape.** Establish licensed data access, venue scope, event integrity, and deterministic reconstruction before defining hidden-liquidity or tape thresholds.
3. **Realistic execution.** Add marketable-limit semantics, latency, partial fills, cancellation acknowledgements, queue uncertainty, halts, fees, and impact.
4. **Integrated management.** Preselect a management interpretation before prospective results and recompute account chronology.
5. **Portfolio validation.** Complete the registered August 24–September 4 run, then a larger walk-forward and live shadow sequence.

## Authority boundary

- Deterministic code remains responsible for data, timestamps, calculations, orders, and risk.
- AI remains evidence-cited, expiring, abstaining, and shadow-only.
- Raw transcripts remain offline retrospective evidence.
- The matrix cannot authorize a strategy, select a best cell, or support a Ross-equivalence or profitability claim.
