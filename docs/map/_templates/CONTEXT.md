# Map card templates

Inputs: [schema](../_meta/schema.md) and the source being mapped.
Process: copy [object.md](object.md) or [process.md](process.md); replace placeholders,
review actual source, and change stub to verified only with citations and evidence.
Outputs: a new card in the corresponding sibling folder.
Human check: confirm the card describes implemented behavior and first-order effects.
Blank templates are factory material, never completed map records.
